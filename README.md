🚛 Inbound Supplier Performance & TCO DiagnosticUncovering Hidden Financial Leakage in Offshore Sourcing Networks📌 Executive Summary (BLUF)An end-to-end diagnostic of 10,000 Purchase Orders (POs) across 15 global suppliers revealed a critical operational breakdown: an Inbound OTIF (On-Time In-Full) of just 36.93%, which triggered $5.29M USD in emergency expedited freight costs.While Contract Purchase Prices ($PPV$) suggested that Asian sourcing was up to 40% cheaper than local alternatives, the severe lead time volatility ($\sigma = 18$ days) from offshore suppliers repeatedly forced the operation into air freight contingencies to prevent production stoppages.A Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) evaluation demonstrated that 94.3% of the financial leakage originated in Asia, eroding nominal contract savings. To resolve this, a two-phase strategic proposal was designed: a 60/40 Dual-Sourcing transition to Nearshoring (USA/Mexico) to reclaim $3.5M USD in annual freight waste, followed by a Vendor Development Program to elevate Nearshoring OTIF from 76% to world-class benchmarks (>90%).🗂️ Project Architecture & Workflowinbound-supplier-performance-diagnostic/
